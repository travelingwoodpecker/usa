July 22–29, 2023 · Motorcycle ride

New Hampshire

Straight from Norway to Boston, then north with the bikes to the White Mountains: Mount Washington, Jackson and the covered bridges, the North Country up to Colebrook, a round of golf at Bretton Woods, and a few days on Cape Cod and in Boston to finish.

Arrive · Sat, Jul 22

Boston

In from Oslo for a night in Boston before heading north.

Day 1 · Sun, Jul 23

North to the White Mountains

The bikes went north by van, and we rode from there into the White Mountains.

Day 2 · Mon, Jul 24

Mount Washington and the North Country

Through the Honeymoon Covered Bridge in Jackson and up to the summit of Mount Washington, then north along the Connecticut River before returning by way of Bretton Woods.

Day 3 · Tue, Jul 25

Franconia and golf at Bretton Woods

A short ride around Franconia and Sugar Hill, then a round of golf in front of the Mount Washington Hotel.

Day 4 · Wed, Jul 26

South to Tilton and back by the covered bridges

Breakfast at the Tilt’n Diner, then back north by way of the Bath Covered Bridge.

After the ride · Jul 27–29

Cape Cod and Boston

Golf at Eastward Ho! in Chatham on Cape Cod, then back to Boston.
