Day 1 · Sat, Oct 4

San Francisco Ukiah, CA

~150 mi · 4 hrs

Scenic ride via US-101 and CA-1 north, mixing coastal beauty, redwoods and small towns.

Along the way

  • Golden Gate Bridge — North Vista Point photos
  • Marin Headlands — bridge and coast views; winding roads
  • Stinson Beach — beach and lagoon stop
  • Point Reyes National Seashore — cliffs and waves; optional lighthouse detour (~20 mi)
  • Tomales Bay — bay views and oyster farms
  • Bodega Bay — harbor, seafood, “The Birds” film site; exhilarating CA-1 curves
  • Bodega Dunes — windswept trails
  • Sebastopol — orchards and farm stands via CA-12 E
  • Santa Rosa Historic District — tree-lined streets
  • Kendall-Jackson Wine Estate & Gardens — tasting and gardens (~7 mi from Santa Rosa)
  • US-101 N — through Mendocino vineyards
  • Hendy Woods State Park — redwood detour on CA-128
  • Ukiah Valley — Mayacamas Mountains at sunset

Riding tips

Early morning or late afternoon for golden light and less fog. CA-1 is tight and thrilling but watch for fog and wind; US-101 is faster and straighter. Plan one or two stops (Stinson Beach, Bodega Bay, Kendall-Jackson) on the ~3 hr 53 min ride. Fuel at Bodega — stations are sparse on CA-1.

Day 2 · Sun, Oct 5

Ukiah Crescent City, CA

~240 mi · 4.5 hrs

The Redwood Highway (US-101 north) through forests, coast and towns.

Along the way

  • Ukiah wine country — vineyards and oaks
  • Willits — “Gateway to the Redwoods”
  • Laytonville — redwood canopy
  • Avenue of the Giants — 31 mi through Humboldt Redwoods SP; Founders Grove, Rockefeller Loop
  • Garberville / Scotia — Eel River views
  • Eureka — Victorian town; Old Town marina detour
  • Arcata Bay / Humboldt Lagoons — wetlands, ocean vistas; elk at Elk Prairie
  • Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park — meadows and redwoods
  • Del Norte Coast Redwoods State Park — ocean pullouts
  • Trees of Mystery, Klamath — Paul Bunyan statue and gondola
  • Battery Point Lighthouse — sunset tides

Riding tips

4–5 hours without stops; plan 6–8 with sights. Tight curves in the redwoods and along the coast; fog and wet roads are common in the morning. Fuel in Ukiah, Willits or Eureka (scarce in the redwoods). Watch for elk and deer.

Day 3 · Mon, Oct 6

Crescent City Klamath Falls, OR

~200 mi · 4 hrs

US-199 (the Redwood Highway) to Grants Pass, OR-238 through the Applegate Valley to Medford, then OR-140 to Klamath Falls.

Along the way

  • Jedediah Smith Redwoods SP — Stout Grove detour on Howland Hill Rd
  • Smith River — turquoise water and cliffs; Patrick Creek Overlook
  • Collier Tunnel — winding and narrow
  • Applegate Valley — vineyards and orchards; Applegate Lake stop
  • Oregon Caves National Monument — optional OR-46 detour (~20 mi) to marble caves
  • Fish Lake / Lake of the Woods — mountain lakes on OR-140
  • Crater Lake National Park — optional, ~70 mi round trip; Rim Drive vistas

Riding tips

3.5–4 hours base; 5–7 with detours. Curvy and twisty on US-199 and OR-46; low traffic on OR-238. Fuel in Grants Pass or Medford. Bring layers for the coastal chill and inland warmth.

Day 4 · Tue, Oct 7

Klamath Falls Red Bluff, CA

~250 mi · 4.5 hrs

US-97 south to Mount Shasta, CA-89 south and CA-44 through Lassen Volcanic National Park to Redding, then I-5 south to Red Bluff.

Along the way

  • Upper Klamath Lake — wetlands and pelicans
  • Klamath Basin grasslands — open vistas
  • Mount Shasta — vista point near Weed; optional CA-89 detour via McCloud for river and falls
  • Burney Falls — 129-ft cascade in the state park
  • Hat Creek Valley — lava fields and Lassen Peak views
  • Lassen Volcanic NP — Manzanita Lake, Sulphur Works, Bumpass Hell, Kings Creek / Summit Lake
  • Shingletown — oak woodlands
  • Redding — Sacramento River and Sundial Bridge
  • Red Bluff — Victorian charm on the river

Riding tips

6–8 hours with stops. Thrilling CA-89 switchbacks; bring layers for the elevation swings. Fuel in Klamath Falls, Weed, Burney or Redding.

Day 5 · Wed, Oct 8

Red Bluff Sonoma, CA

~180 mi · 3.5 hrs

I-5 south to Williams, CA-20 west to Clearlake, CA-53 and CA-29 south to Calistoga, then CA-12 west to Sonoma.

Along the way

  • Sacramento Valley farms — almond and rice fields, Sierra views
  • CA-20 to Clear Lake — rolling hills and oaks; Mount Konocti volcano
  • CA-53 — lake views and Lower Lake towns
  • Napa Valley on CA-29 — vineyards, Mayacamas Mountains, balloons, Calistoga springs
  • CA-12 — oak hills and horse ranches to Sonoma Plaza

Riding tips

Balances straight I-5 cruising with CA-20 and CA-12 curves. Go early for fewer tourists. Watch for winery traffic and cyclists on CA-29 and CA-12.
