October 8–14, 2022 · Motorcycle tour

Las Vegas, Utah & Arizona

Six days and about 1,650 miles through southern Utah’s parks and back roads, from Kolob Terrace to Bull Creek Pass, Moki Dugway to Hell’s Backbone country, with the Grand Canyon’s North Rim and Zion to finish. We all agreed it was one of the best motorcycle trips ever.

St. George, UT

Conveniently on St. George Boulevard with easy access from I-15. Basic, adequate rooms, a cozy lobby for a late-night meeting and breakfast, and a welcoming front desk happy to share local knowledge. Café Sabor, a decent Mexican restaurant, is right next door.

Day 1 · Sun, Oct 9

St. George, UT Torrey, UT

259 mi · 6 hr 11 min (about 10 hours actual)

Kolob Terrace Road out of Virgin was a spectacular start: paved as far as Cedar Canyon Campground, then dirt up to Highway 14. We met 800 sheep on the way!

Past Cedar Breaks National Monument, lunch in Panguitch, then the magnificent Sunset Point in Bryce Canyon National Park before Highway 12 through Grand Staircase–Escalante.

Day 2 · Mon, Oct 10

Torrey, UT Moab, UT

269 mi · 6 hr 34 min (about 10 hours actual)

Another epic day. A leisurely ride down the scenic drive from the Capitol Reef visitor center, then a splendid road to Notom and over Bull Creek Pass to Hanksville and Moab.

Unpaved Bull Creek Pass was quite challenging and became the group’s favorite ride, though it tested anyone short on off-road skills: there were drops and spills, and a mid-way decision on whether to turn back. We pushed through, and it was the most adventurous and rewarding day.

A great Italian dinner at Il Posto Rosso, right next to the Red Stone Inn. The food was exceptional.

Overnight

Red Stone Inn, Moab

Day 3 · Tue, Oct 11

Moab, UT Bluff, UT

194 mi

UT-128 is a beautiful road along the Colorado River, followed by the La Sal Loop Road. After a taco lunch in Moab, south to Bluff.

The entry into Bluff was grand, riding between two great boulders to the Bluff Dwellings Resort & Spa. A very good dinner at the Twin Rocks Cafe: Mexican-style food on fry bread.

Overnight

Bluff Dwellings Resort & Spa

Day 4 · Wed, Oct 12

Bluff, UT Boulder, UT

~300 mi

Valley of the Gods and the Moki Dugway, then Ticaboo to fuel up for the Burr Trail. A section of the Burr Trail was impassable after recent rain washed it out, so we turned back north to Hanksville and took UT-24. It was a fortunate detour: UT-24 was beautiful, and UT-12 from Torrey to Boulder was spectacular.

Hell’s Backbone Grill was a great find, wonderful food at the edge of the wilderness. The next morning’s breakfast at Annie’s Place, oven-baked pancakes, was incredible.

Day 5 · Thu, Oct 13

Boulder, UT Grand Canyon North Rim

147 mi to Kanab · 81 mi Kanab to the North Rim

A treat to ride Grand Staircase–Escalante again. From Cannonville, Cottonwood Road past Kodachrome Basin to Kanab, then down to the Grand Canyon North Rim visitor center in the late afternoon.

Overnight

Day 6 · Fri, Oct 14

Kanab, UT Las Vegas, NV

256 mi · 6 hr 9 min

Through Zion National Park, which was spectacular, more intimate and personal than the Grand Canyon. Then down to Las Vegas avoiding I-15 as much as possible; Valley of Fire State Park was a beautiful surprise at the end of the trip.

A feast at Milos, the Greek restaurant in the Venetian, to close out an intense six days.

Overnight

Resorts World / The Palazzo at the Venetian, Las Vegas
